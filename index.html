A note on transient price impact.

Trading moves the market against the trader, but what is the temporal nature of this effect? In models of price impact, large trades are often associated with permanent price impact, while smaller trades are typically modeled as having only instantaneous effects. In reality, however, the truth likely lies somewhere in between. Price impact is often better described as transient: trades move prices, but this effect then gradually decays, with prices partially reverting toward their pre-trade level over time.
Nagy, L. & Rásonyi, M. (2025). On the utility problem in a market where price impact is transient. arXiv:2511.12093 [arxiv]
